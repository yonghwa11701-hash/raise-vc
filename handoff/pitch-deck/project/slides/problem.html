OUR BELIEF

Finding an investor should take a pitch, not a network.

RAISE

02

[0:10–0:30] Raising in London today means networking. Founders spend months of evenings at events, hoping to meet the right partner. The investor lists that exist are scattered, out of date or behind a paywall. And if you’re a young founder with no network, you’re filtered out before anyone hears your idea.
